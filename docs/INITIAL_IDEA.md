I am interested in pursuing the following hackathon track:
Resurrection: Bring your favorite dead technology back to life. Reimagine obsolete tech with today’s innovations or solving tomorrow's problems.

One technology that I found was the RSS reader, which seems to be how news was distributed before the age of social media and algorithms. Google Reader is a discontinued web-based RSS and Atom feed aggregator that was launched in 2005 and discontinued in 2013. It allowed users to subscribe to blogs and news sites with one centralized dashboard. 

While RSS technology is not fully "dead" and still exists today, I am interested in building a project that uses it while branding the UI with throwback, 8-bit terminal aesthetics. 

The application should be low-stimulation, feel like an old device, and takes us back to a time before our feeds are populated with short-form video content. 

I do want to build an algorithm to suggest content, tailoring the news feed to the content that the user is most engaged with.
